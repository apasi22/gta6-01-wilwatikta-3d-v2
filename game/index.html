Open-world crime in a Javanese sunset city

Sugeng Wibowo drives, walks, steals, shoots and outruns the WPD across six districts — 12 story missions, police chases, a helicopter, roadblocks, a garage, gamelan radio and three save slots. All original. All procedural. No network.

HOW TO PLAY

Drive with WASD, steer with A/D, handbrake with SPACE. Walk with WASD, sprint with Shift. Press E near any car to steal and drive it. Shoot with the mouse or SPACE, punch with F, reload with R, switch weapons with Q or 1/2/3. Follow the gold route to the beacon to finish each mission. Lose the police by breaking line of sight — the helicopter stops your wanted level from falling. Press G at Bengkel Bambang for upgrades, Tab for the city map, T to change the radio station.
