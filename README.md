# WILWATIKTA 3D

An original open-world crime game in a Javanese sunset city, built from scratch
for make.fun's gta6-01. Sugeng Wibowo — getaway driver, new in town — runs
12 story missions across six districts, outrunning WPD chases, roadblocks and
the police helicopter, while Agus Prasetyo, Komisaris Wanto and Bambang pull
him deeper into Wilwatikta's gold-and-neon underworld.

## What is in the game

- **A real 3D city**: a from-scratch raw-WebGL renderer (no libraries, no
  assets, zero network) — dense facades with small-window grids, storefronts
  and neon signs, roof props, palm-lined beachfront avenue, sea, sand,
  mountains and a fog-faded skyline, day/dusk/night light.
- **Driving and walking**: six stealable car models with distinct handling,
  traffic, parked cars, pedestrians and beachgoers, collisions and damage.
- **Police**: wanted stars 0–5, pursuit cars with flashing light bars,
  roadblocks, and a helicopter with a searchlight that stops your heat falling.
- **Combat**: fists, pistol and SMG, reloads, enemy thugs, health/armor/ammo
  pickups, muzzle flashes and smoke.
- **Story**: 12 missions with numbered banners, timers, dialogue, a gold route
  line and a 3D beacon — deliveries, thefts, races, escapes and a finale.
- **Progression**: cash, reputation ranks, Bengkel Bambang garage upgrades
  (engine, armor, paint), three save slots.
- **Presentation**: full HUD with a colourful minimap and speedometer, a
  full-city map screen, title/pause/garage/how-to/passed/busted/wasted screens,
  two synthesized radio stations and sound effects.

## Controls

WASD drive / walk · E enter or exit car · SPACE shoot / handbrake · F punch ·
R reload · Q weapon · T radio · G garage · Tab map · P pause · M mute · H horn.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Staged screenshots:
`?shot=cover|map|foot|night&seed=1`.
